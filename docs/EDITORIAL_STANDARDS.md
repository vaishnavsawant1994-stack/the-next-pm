# Editorial standards — The Next PM

**Status:** Foundational policy draft, subject to formal ownership and Indian legal review.

## Public commitments
- Independent, nonpartisan and source-first. Do not endorse a candidate or political party; do not generate rankings, overall evaluations or instructions on how to vote.
- Do not imply affiliation with the Election Commission of India, a government department or a political campaign.
- No simulated official online election; a public survey is never an official vote or necessarily representative.
- Do not infer or profile a visitor's political preference, caste, religion or other sensitive traits.
- Disclose ownership, material conflicts of interest and clearly labeled financial sponsorship before enabling monetization.

## Source hierarchy
1. Official ECI notices, published candidate affidavits and official results/Forms 20.
2. Official public records and published party manifestos for their own positions.
3. Sourced reporting, always explicitly attributed and dated.
4. Reviewed, explanatory editorial content with citations.

Each published factual claim should carry an original link or a traceable citation record. Store capture and publication timestamps, source author/publisher, document version, licence/right-to-use status and reviewer.

## Election date and result labels
- **Expected:** ordinary legislative terms or planning assumption, *not* an ECI poll notice.
- **Announced:** verified ECI notification or official schedule with URL and date.
- **Provisional:** live counting information; subject to revision.
- **Final:** approved official final documents and archived revision.
Unknown values remain unknown, never zero.

## Sensitive claims and corrections
- Attribute public claims, quote in context and distinguish charges, ongoing proceedings, acquittal and conviction.
- Affidavit fields are the candidate's filed declarations, not independent verification.
- Substantive public corrections should be logged, with date, old/new wording and source.
- Provide a right-to-reply and dispute workflow before publishing contested sensitive material.
- Avoid fabricated quotations, deepfakes, impersonation and manipulative political media.

## Launch controls
User-generated discussion, political advertising, exit/opinion polls, AI-generated political content and live results publishing are disabled until their respective legal, safety and human-review gates are implemented and approved.
